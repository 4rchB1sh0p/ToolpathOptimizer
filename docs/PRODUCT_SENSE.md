# Product sense

Status: Hypothesis (unvalidated) | Last reviewed: 2026-11-19

## Purpose and scope
- Who the product is for, the job it does and what it must avoid.

## Key points
- **Buyer**: CNC programming lead or manufacturing engineer at a small precision machining shop with repeated point-to-point drilling patterns.
- **Job**: compare the current order with a constraint-respecting alternative and decide whether a CAM programmer should review a change.
- **Value hypothesis**: less manual sequencing review; visible travel-distance differences. No cycle-time or cost-saving claims without measured shop data.
- **Avoid**: complete CAM, cutter engagement, post-processing, 5-axis kinematics, live controller integration, collision certification, automated machine execution.
- **Validation**: five buyer interviews; public forum research (e.g. CNCzone, Practical Machinist); synthetic demo; ask for a paid pilot commitment.
- **Pricing hypothesis**: €99/month/team — not validated.

## Acceptance criteria
- Interview notes (anonymised, no customer IP) recorded in plan 0001 before Core build starts.
- At least one pilot commitment before web billing work.

## Risks
- Problem too small for willingness to pay → stop or pivot after interviews.
- CAM vendors already provide sequencing → interviews must compare.
